About me

Hello again!

I’m the Myspace millennial who customized layouts, embedded music, and broke things on purpose just to understand how they worked. That same curiosity still drives how I learn and build today.

I don’t chase quick fixes. I trace problems to their source because durable understanding matters more than patching symptoms. It slows me down at first, but it makes me faster and more reliable in the long run. I document what I learn so I can build on it instead of relearning under pressure.

Working alongside modern AI tools has reinforced that mindset. I don’t treat outputs as answers. I evaluate whether they actually make sense in the context. For me, development is a practice of turning confusion into structure. One intentional iteration at a time.

Outside of coding, you can find me bellydancing, practicing archery, or sewing a new garment.
